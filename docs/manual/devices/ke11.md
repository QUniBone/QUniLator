The **KE11-A** is the extended arithmetic element of the PDP-11/20: a UNIBUS
card that multiplies, divides, shifts and normalizes 32-bit values for a
processor whose instruction set has no instructions for it. A program writes an
operand to one of its registers and reads the result back. QUniLator lists it as
`ke`, of type `KE11`.

> **UNIBUS · UniBone**
>
> The KE11-A is a UNIBUS card. QUniLator builds it for the UniBone only.

## What the machine sees

Eight registers from 777300. AC (the high word) and MQ (the low word) hold a
32-bit value. Writing an operand to DIV, MUL, LSH or ASH carries out that
operation on it, and writing NOR normalizes it; the result is left in AC and MQ,
and the status in SR.

| Address | Register | |
|---|---|---|
| 777300 | DIV | Divide AC and MQ by the word written: quotient in MQ, remainder in AC. Reads 0 |
| 777302 | AC | Accumulator, the high word |
| 777304 | MQ | Multiplier-quotient, the low word. Writing it sign-extends it into AC |
| 777306 | MUL | Multiply MQ by the word written, the product in AC and MQ. Reads 0 |
| 777310 | SC/SR | Step counter in the low byte, status in the high byte |
| 777312 | NOR | Writing it normalizes AC and MQ, counting the shifts in the step counter |
| 777314 | LSH | Logical shift of AC and MQ by the count written. Reads 0 |
| 777316 | ASH | Arithmetic shift of AC and MQ by the count written. Reads 0 |

A byte written to the low half of AC, MQ, DIV or MUL is sign-extended to a word.
INIT and power-up clear every register. The card takes no interrupt.

## Using it

Enable `ke` on the **Configurations** screen. It has no settings of its own, and
`base_addr` moves it away from 777300.

## What has been verified

No DEC diagnostic is recorded as passing against this card.
